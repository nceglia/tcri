## 0.15.0 (2026-11-06)

### Breaking changes

- `tcri.null.*` fits `n_nulls=5` nulls per reference, `null.<kind>[i]`, and scored metrics pool their draws with all of them; a null's permutation no longer depends on the fits already present. ([#269](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/269))
- `tcri.null.condition` is now `tcri.null.covariate`, and `simulate_cohort` takes `timepoints=` and writes `obs["timepoint"]`. ([#270](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/270))
- In the scored `tl` metrics, `null_model` now follows `fit`. ([#272](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/272))
- `tl` tools and `perturb.gene_importance` store their result and return `None`; MI and the entropies store `{replicate, item, stats}`; `tcri.get.table` and the `tcri.get.<tool>` readers are removed. ([#273](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/273))
- `to_anndata(compute_umap=True)` is removed, along with the umap-learn dependency; embed `obsm["X_tcri"]` with `sc.pp.neighbors(use_rep="X_tcri")` and `sc.tl.umap`. ([#274](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/274))
- `diag` checks return dicts of named frames and draw with `plot=True`; `joint_distribution_ppc` is a sampled-posterior check (`n_samples`, `n_sims`); `reconstruction_ppc` is unseeded by default. ([#277](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/277))
- Gene importance stores only its adjusted value: read `tcri.get.replicates`/`items`, not `["result"]`/`["shift"]`; `pl.gene_importance(kind=)` is `view="replicate"|"item"`; joint `sample_id` is `draw`. ([#278](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/278))
- `pp` summaries store their result and return `None`, drawn by `pl` twins; sharing and persistence store pairwise frames (no `cov_from`, `per_clone`); `repertoire_summary` adds `clonality`. ([#279](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/279))
- MI and the entropies take `cov_from`/`cov_to` for a change, with a paired test; flux is stored by level; `tl`/`pl.delta_*` are removed; a generator `random_state` is recorded as its seed. ([#280](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/280))
- `to_anndata` returns `None`; `print=` is on only for setup, `train`, `null.*`; `rich`, `ipywidgets` required; scvi bar args and bar callbacks raise; `module=` filters miss tcri warnings. ([#287](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/287))

### Documentation

- The concepts page explains rare clones and sampling depth, and how a fit ends and how to read `training_record_`. ([#267](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/267))

### Features

- `pp.exclude_tcr_genes` leaves the TCR V, D and J segment genes out of the fitted genes, keeping the invariant MAIT, iNKT and Vγ9Vδ2 segments; `setup_anndata` warns when they are registered. ([#266](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/266))
- `tcri.datasets.yost2019` downloads the Yost et al. (2019) BCC/SCC T-cell dataset from GEO and caches it as a scirpy MuData for `tcri.pp.from_mudata`. Install with `tcri[datasets]`. ([#282](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/282))
- `pl.joint_distribution(view="sankey")` draws the phenotype-flow Sankey across covariate values from the stored joint distribution; `normalize` picks fractions or cell counts. ([#284](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/284))
- `tcri.pl` colors start with green, yellow and red, and one-color marks are green; `pl.gene_importance(view="replicate")` colors its dots by group, never by gene. ([#285](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/285))

### Bug fixes

- A `stats` contrast with an undefined p has blank `stars`, not `"ns"`; a frame with more than one row per replicate and level is refused. ([#271](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/271))
- On MPS, training no longer restarts its random draws from the validation seed every epoch; seeded checks and draws restore the CPU and device generators they seed. ([#289](https://github.com/nceglia/tcri/pull/289))
