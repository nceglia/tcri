# The model

`TCRIModel` implements the generative model of **Supplementary Note 1** — a
hierarchical Bayesian model, fit with [Pyro](https://pyro.ai) on top of an
[scvi-tools](https://scvi-tools.org) variational autoencoder, that ties each clone's
phenotype distribution to gene expression. This page is the conceptual walk-through; the
mathematics are frozen by the [model contract](../contracts/index.md), and the
eq-by-eq code map lives in `docs/contract/METHODS_CONFORMANCE.md`.

```{note}
Equation numbers here refer to **Supplementary Note 1**. The metrics document numbers
its own equations independently — "eq 3" is the VampPrior here but the clonotypic entropy
there — so every reference names its document.
```

Shaded nodes are **observed** (counts $X_i$, phenotype labels $\text{Pheno}_i$); coloured
nodes are **latent** ($p_c$, $p_{ct}$, $z_i$, $z_{i,\text{phen}}$); the small grey circles

nested plates are the three index sets of the [data model](data-model.md): clonotypes $c$,
clone × covariate pairs $ct$, and cells $i$.

## The generative story (eqs 1–5)

The joint factorizes as

$$p(\Omega, \Phi, z, x) = \prod_c p(\omega_c)\; \prod_m p(\phi_m \mid \omega_{h(m)})\;
\prod_i p(z_i)\, p(z^\phi_i \mid z_i, \phi_{g(i)})\, p(x_i \mid z_i).$$

| eq | site | distribution | plate | meaning |
|----|------|--------------|-------|---------|
| 1 | `p_c` | MixtureDirichlet | clonotypes | $\omega_c \sim \tfrac{1}{B_c}\sum_b \mathrm{Dir}(\alpha\psi_b)$ — the clone's phenotype distribution over archetypes $\psi_b$ |
| 2 | `p_ct` | Dirichlet | clone × covariate | $\phi_m \mid \omega_{h(m)} \sim \mathrm{Dir}(\beta\,\omega_{h(m)})$ — covariate-level, hierarchical under its clone |
| 3 | `latent` | VampPrior mixture | cells | $z_i \sim \tfrac{1}{B_z}\sum_k q(z\mid u_k)$ over learnable pseudo-inputs $u_k$ |
| 4 | *(surrogate)* | — | cells | $\ell_i = \pi\, f_\text{cls}(z_i) + (1-\pi)\log\phi_{g(i)}$; $z^\phi_i \sim \mathrm{Cat}(\operatorname{softmax}\ell_i)$ |
| 5 | `obs` | ZeroInflatedNegativeBinomial | cells | $x_i \sim \mathrm{ZINB}$ from the scVI decoder |

**Scales are semantics, not tuning.** $\alpha$ (`global_scale`, default `5.0`) scales
eq 1's concentration and $\beta$ (`local_scale`, default `3.0`) scales eq 2's. Dropping
either changes the prior's *shape* — with concentration entries below 1 a Dirichlet turns
U-shaped (mass at the simplex corners), the opposite of a prior peaked at the archetype.

## The objective (eqs 6–7)

The variational family (eq 6) places a Dirichlet on each `p_c` and `p_ct`, and a Gaussian
encoder on each `z_i`. The ELBO (eq 7),

$$\mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}_q[\log p(x\mid z)] + \mathbb{E}_q[\log p(\Omega,\Phi,z,z^\phi)]
- \mathbb{E}_q[\log q],$$

is maximized by SVI (Adam, reparameterized continuous latents).

### The phenotype surrogate

The discrete $z^\phi$ terms are replaced by a differentiable **alignment penalty** that
pulls the classifier's per-cell distribution toward the clone's local prior:

$$\mathcal{L}_\text{new} = \mathcal{L}^\# + \gamma \sum_i
\mathrm{KL}\!\big(\operatorname{softmax}(\ell_i)\;\|\;\phi_{g(i)}\big).$$

Because the note *penalizes* misalignment, the KL is minimized; since Pyro **maximizes**
the log-joint, it is registered with a minus sign — `pyro.factor("phenotype_alignment",
-γ·kl)`. This surrogate is **the only thing that trains the classifier** `f_cls`: without
it the logits never enter the ELBO and phenotype recovery sits at chance. $\gamma$ is
`phenotype_kl_weight` (default `1.0`).

## Gating (π)

The per-cell phenotype fuses two sources of evidence — the classifier on $z$ and the
clone's local prior — through a gate $\pi$ = `gate_prob` (default **0.5**):

$$P(\phi \mid \text{cell}) = \operatorname{softmax}\!\big(\pi\, f_\text{cls}(z) +
(1-\pi)\log p_{ct}\big).$$

The endpoints are contract-tested: $\pi=1$ makes `predict()` the pure classifier,
$\pi=0$ the pure clonotype prior, and `gate_prob=None` recovers the additive rule
$f_\text{cls} + \log p_{ct}$.

## Sanctioned deviations from the note

A handful of departures are accepted and recorded in `SANCTIONED_DEVIATIONS`
(`tcri/model/_model_contract.py`); anything **not** listed there that departs from the
note is a defect.

| departure | rationale |
|-----------|-----------|
| `obs` reconstruction scaled by `reconstruction_loss_scale` (default `1e-2`) | β-VAE-style reweighting; re-measured so the posterior-predictive library ratio ≈ 1 on real data |
| KL warmup anneals only the `latent` KL | standard annealing; training-only, not part of eq 7 |
| in-silico perturbation (eqs 8–12) not implemented | additive feature, out of scope for this release |
| Adam weight decay on the network parameters | the note fixes the objective, not the optimizer |

See [The contracts](../contracts/index.md) and `MODEL_CONTRACT.md` for the full rationale
and the behavioural checks that enforce each site, plate and scale.
